How does the pipeline run?

Pick how the Job Hunter operates in the background once a campaign is active.

A

Non-stop 24/7

Campaign runs continuously — scrapes, tailors, applies, monitors email all the time.

Pros

  • First to apply to new postings
  • Email replies within minutes

Cons

  • Higher proxy cost
  • Higher bot-detection risk
B

Scheduled (e.g. every 6 hours)

Campaign runs on a set schedule. Scrapes, applies, checks email in batches. Quiet between runs.

Pros

  • Controlled, predictable
  • Lower cost & ban risk

Cons

  • Email replies can be hours late
  • Slower to catch new postings
C

Scheduled applying + always-on email Recommended

Job scraping + applying runs every 6 hours. Email monitoring runs 24/7 — interview invites and rejection replies go out within minutes, always.

Pros

  • Fast email response (most time-sensitive)
  • Controlled scraping = lower cost + safer
  • Best balance overall

Cons

  • Not the absolute fastest to apply

Click your choice or just say it in the terminal.
